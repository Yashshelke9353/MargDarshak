Instructions, Rules, Start and Conclusion
Instructions (Quick Flow)

Follow this sequence to avoid confusion and start GD smoothly.

  • Create meeting, set topic, duration, and participant limit.
  • Invite students or share room code/link.
  • Host starts timer when everyone is ready.
GD Rules

These rules improve communication score and discussion quality.

  • Do not interrupt; respect each speaker.
  • Stay on topic; avoid repeating points.
  • Use Point -> Reason -> Example for clarity.
Start and Conclude

A strong opening and ending can lift your GD impression quickly.

  • Start: define topic and give your direction in 20 seconds.
  • Middle: support 1-2 key points with examples.
  • Conclude: summarize both sides and give one final takeaway.
