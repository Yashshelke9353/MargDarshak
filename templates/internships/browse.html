How to Apply

Search & Research

Find internships that match your field and interests. Research company culture and requirements.

Prepare Documents

Update resume, cover letter, and portfolio. Tailor each application to the specific role.

Apply Smartly

Use professional templates and proofread everything. Apply early and follow instructions.

Follow Up

Send polite follow-ups after 1-2 weeks. Connect on LinkedIn with hiring managers.

Prepare & Interview

Practice common questions, prepare questions for them, and showcase your enthusiasm.

Resume Tips

  • Length: Keep to 1 page
  • Content: Include education, experience, skills, projects
  • Format: Clean, professional, easy to read
  • Keywords: Use industry-specific terms
  • Quantify: Show impact with numbers
  • Proofread: No spelling errors

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Generic applications: Don't send the same resume everywhere
  • Poor research: Know about the company and role
  • Late applications: Apply as soon as positions open
  • Weak follow-ups: Be professional, not pushy
  • Missing documents: Ensure all requirements are met
  • Negative attitude: Stay positive and enthusiastic

Networking Tips

  • LinkedIn: Connect with professionals in your field
  • Alumni network: Reach out to college seniors
  • Industry events: Attend webinars and virtual meetups
  • Informational interviews: Learn about different roles
  • Professional groups: Join relevant online communities
  • Referrals: Ask for introductions when appropriate

Research Company Tips

  • Website: Read about mission, values, recent news
  • Social media: Follow company updates and culture
  • Glassdoor: Check reviews and interview experiences
  • News: Stay updated on industry trends
  • Products/Services: Understand what they offer
  • Competitors: Know the industry landscape

Interview Preparation

  • Practice questions: "Tell me about yourself," "Why this company?"
  • Prepare examples: STAR method for behavioral questions
  • Know your resume: Be ready to discuss any item
  • Research salary: Know market rates for your field
  • Ask questions: Show interest in the role and company
  • Follow up: Send thank-you email within 24 hours

Online Presence Tips

  • LinkedIn: Professional profile with photo and summary
  • Portfolio: Showcase projects and achievements
  • GitHub: For technical fields, show code contributions
  • Personal website: Professional online presence
  • Social media: Keep it professional and positive
  • Recommendations: Get endorsements from professors/peers

Success Strategies

  • Apply early: Don't wait for deadlines
  • Quality over quantity: Better applications than many generic ones
  • Track applications: Keep record of where and when you applied
  • Learn from rejections: Ask for feedback when possible
  • Stay persistent: Most internships require multiple applications
  • Build relationships: Networking often leads to opportunities
